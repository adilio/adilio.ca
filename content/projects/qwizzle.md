Qwizzle flips the familiar word-game formula: instead of guessing a random word, you guess the word from its clue, then make the game your own.

- **Daily and random modes** with careful duplicate-letter handling.
- **Bring your own words:** Load custom lists, restyle the game, and publish your own edition.
- **Stats that stick:** Plays, wins, streaks, and hard mode.
- **Share and challenge:** Emoji-grid result cards and direct puzzle links.
