QR codes are on menus, parking meters, and billboards, and you cannot see where any of them go until it is too late. QRCheck gives you a look first.

- **Decodes locally:** Camera or uploaded image, handled entirely in the browser.
- **Answers immediately:** Client-side checks flag suspicious domains, phishing language, shorteners, typosquatting, look-alike characters, and encoded payloads.
- **Follows redirects:** Shortened links are expanded so the final destination is visible before you visit it.

No sign-up, no tracking, and no sending your scan to somebody else's server.
