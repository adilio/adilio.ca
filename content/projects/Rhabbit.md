Rhabbit is a private, mobile-first habit tracker built for daily use. Open it, tap a habit, get on with your day.

- **One-tap logging** with quick undo and numeric steppers for quantity-based goals.
- **Forgiving by design:** Skip days without guilt, pause habits while travelling, and get credit for comebacks.
- **Flexible schedules:** Every day, chosen weekdays, or a number of times per week.
- **Bring your history:** Import from Excel or CSV, preview the result, and export everything whenever you want.

Private to a small circle of friends, with no ads and no paywall—just habits, one hop at a time.
