A complete Secret Santa organizer that handles the awkward parts of a gift exchange so everyone can enjoy the fun ones.

- **Groups and invites** with Google or GitHub sign-in.
- **Smart matching** with exclusions and no self-matches.
- **Required wishlists** so every giver has something to work with.
- **Gift tracking** from idea to mailed to received.

After the draw, each participant only sees their own recipient.
