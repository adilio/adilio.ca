4dl is the home for apps born after finding nothing, finding the wrong thing, or finding the right thing behind seventeen ads and a subscription.

The collection ranges from privacy-first utilities to small social tools and games. Each app stands on its own, but shares a preference for direct language, a useful free core, and interfaces that stay out of the way.

The domain also doubles as a private link shortener: any `4dl.ca/abc123` link points wherever I pointed it.
