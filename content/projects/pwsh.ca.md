pwsh.ca is a small, community-run corner of the internet for people who live in the shell.

It combines a landing place for the open-source PowerShell community with a practical short-link service, while keeping its scope and relationship to Microsoft explicit.

The project is open source and intended to grow alongside the community it serves.
