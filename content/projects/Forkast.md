Recipe sites bury four useful lines under a childhood memoir and seventeen ads. Forkast keeps the four lines.

- **Capture from anywhere:** Paste a recipe URL and get a reviewable draft—ingredients, steps, and times, nothing saved until you've looked at it.
- **Scale for the table:** Change the serving count and every quantity follows. Ambiguous amounts are left intact rather than given invented precision.
- **Store-aware lists:** Each household defines its own stores, and ingredients remember which one they belong to.
- **Works in the aisle:** An installable PWA that stays readable and editable on bad grocery-store signal, then syncs when it returns.
- **Yours to leave with:** Recipes export as schema.org JSON-LD; the whole household exports as JSON.

Private by household: sign in with Google and your recipes stay yours. No ads, no paywall, no recipe blog.
