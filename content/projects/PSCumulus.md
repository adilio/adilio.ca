A thin, honest PowerShell abstraction for Azure, AWS, and GCP.

PSCumulus explores how much of the cloud-provider experience can be made coherent without pretending the underlying platforms are interchangeable. It offers a deliberately narrow set of commands, normalized records, and cross-cloud pipelines while leaving provider-specific seams visible.

The module is also the companion project for the PowerShell + DevOps Global Summit 2026 session, **“Cross-Cloud without Crossed Fingers: Surviving Azure, AWS, and GCP with PowerShell.”**
