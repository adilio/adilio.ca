It answers one question well: *what World Cup matches are happening at my stadium, what's the score, and when is the next kickoff?*

- **Stadium selector:** City-led labels, plus an all-stadiums view.
- **Live scores:** An estimated match clock, penalty results, and a clear live or next-match focus.
- **Knockout bracket:** Round of 32 through the Final, resolving as results come in.
- **Dual time zones:** Every match shows both your time and the venue's local time.

Static-first and live-enhanced: no login, no database, free APIs only.
