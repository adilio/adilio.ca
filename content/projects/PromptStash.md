A fast, clean home for the prompts you actually reuse, because the good ones deserve better than a notes app.

- **Organize:** Hierarchical folders, tags, and full-text search across titles and prompt content.
- **Write in Markdown:** Live preview, token and word counts, and automatic version tracking.
- **Collaborate:** Teams with owner, editor, and viewer roles, invitations, and public read-only sharing.
- **Integrate:** A REST API plus export as an Espanso text-expansion package.
